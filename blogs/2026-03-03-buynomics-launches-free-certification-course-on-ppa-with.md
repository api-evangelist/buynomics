Buynomics has announced the next course in its RGM Academy, partnering with Nestlé's Sr. Director of Strategic RGM, Colin McQuay, to launch a new, expert-led program focused on Price Pack Architecture (PPA). The initiative is designed to make high-quality, practical RGM education accessible at a time when most advanced training formats remain costly and limited to a small group of leaders.
