Buynomics has received three 2026 Best-in-Class Category Distinctions from the Promotion Optimization Institute (POI) in the POI 2026 Consumer Goods Enterprise Planning & Retail Execution Vendor Panorama. The POI Best-in-Class Category Distinctions highlight functionality differentiators that POI considers critical to CPG manufacturers. In 2026, Buynomics has been recognized in three categories: ● Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning ● Consumer Data & Insights Utilization ● RGM Bespoke Solution Buynomics has achieved the Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning and RGM Bespoke Solution
